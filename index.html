How to start the lamp
  1. Switch on the ON/OFF switch on the back of the lamp.
  2. Press the red power button on the remote.
  3. Close the Koran Player app, then press Connect here.
  4. For Quran, books and translations the lamp must be in MP3 (card) mode, not Bluetooth mode: press MP3 (card) in Buttons.

Best: keep the lamp always on and plugged in. To make it quiet, use Pause and "Light on/off" instead of the red power button. When it is off, its Bluetooth is off too and only the remote can wake it.

This browser cannot use Bluetooth. Open this page in Chrome on Android or on a PC. (iPhone browsers do not support Bluetooth web apps.)
